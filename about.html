Romance with Roots

Come stay awhile.

There's always room on the porch.

I believe the best love stories aren't only about finding someone. They're about finding your way back to yourself.

Sadie Lynn writes contemporary romance about people who are figuring things out as they go—people with histories, hopes, bad timing, complicated families, and hearts that don't always cooperate with the plans they've made.

Her stories are rooted in the places that shape us, the friendships that hold us together, and the small moments that turn out to matter far more than we knew.

There may be a little dirt under her fingernails and something growing in the garden, but she'll always leave the porch light on for whoever needs it.

Stories That Find You Back

Because sometimes the way forward starts with remembering who you were.

These are stories about becoming, belonging, and the people who see something in us before we're ready to see it ourselves.
